# Karnataka-first inbound redesign

## Intent

Reposition Gamya Sanchari as a warm, premium Bengaluru-based host for international travellers discovering India, with Karnataka—and Hampi in particular—as the clearest point of entry. Remove the black/blue website theme while preserving the official logo, established enquiry flows, accessibility, motion, audio and responsive behaviour.

## Goals

- Lead the homepage, navigation and metadata with Karnataka and inbound India travel.
- Add Hampi as a first-class destination with original, locally hosted artwork.
- Replace the black/blue interface palette with ivory, sandstone, terracotta, turmeric gold and forest green.
- Keep outbound international journeys available but visually and editorially secondary.
- Preserve current routes, WhatsApp handoff, planner state, reduced-motion behaviour and media fallbacks.

## Non-goals and constraints

- Do not remove current destination or journey pages.
- Do not alter the client-supplied logo colours or the licensed hero montage.
- Do not invent prices, departure dates, testimonials or operational claims.
- Add no new runtime dependency.

## Approach

The chosen approach is a system-wide repositioning rather than a homepage-only reskin: update the shared palette and every remaining hard-coded old-theme colour, add Hampi to the existing destination model, insert a Karnataka gateway chapter near the top of the homepage, and revise hero/navigation/planner/metadata copy. A lighter homepage-only recolour was rejected because inner pages would contradict the brief; removing outbound content was rejected because the user asked to make it secondary, not disappear.

## Experience

The hero introduces “India, beginning in Karnataka” over the existing cinematic montage. The next chapter is a large Hampi-led visual with supporting Karnataka places and practical inbound reassurance. Warm surfaces and deep forest contrast replace blue/navy throughout. International visitors see plain-English planning language, Bengaluru local knowledge, arrival/transfer coordination and a direct planning action.
